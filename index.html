FIELD NOTES

Before you set off

⌖

Explore responsibly

Follow existing park paths, respect heritage structures, and do not enter restricted areas. Check park opening information before visiting and stay on public paths. Photography is optional for the historical chapter quizzes.

◷

Go at your own pace

Each chapter takes about 3–5 minutes to read and answer. In the park, allow extra time for walking and exploring.

✎

A learning prototype

This student concept uses simplified historical summaries. Interactive choices are learning exercises, not reenactments of real historical decisions.
